In-office treatments start change. Your daily routine is what keeps it going.

## The four essentials

A gentle cleanser, a targeted treatment such as a retinoid or pigment corrector, a moisturizer suited to your skin, and daily SPF. Most people do not need more than that.

> We’ll tell you which products you need, and which ones you can skip.

## Professional versus drugstore

Professional lines often contain higher concentrations of active ingredients, backed by clinical testing. That said, we would rather you use an affordable product consistently than an expensive one occasionally.
