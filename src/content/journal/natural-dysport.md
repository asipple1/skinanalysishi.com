Most people who hesitate about wrinkle relaxers are worried about looking frozen. That outcome usually comes from dosing that does not fit the person.

## Dosing for your face

We watch how your face moves before planning injections. Strong muscles need a different approach than fine lines. For first-timers, we start conservatively.

## The two-week check-in

Dysport reaches full effect around two weeks. We see you then to check symmetry and adjust if needed.

> You should still look like yourself on your most expressive day.
