Your first visit is mostly conversation. We want to understand your skin before we touch it.

## What we ask about

Your concerns and goals, medical history and medications, pregnancy or nursing, your current routine, how much time you spend outdoors, your comfort with downtime, and your budget.

## What we look at

We assess your skin type, sensitivity, pigment, texture, and any active concerns. We may take photos so we can track progress accurately.

> You should leave understanding your skin better than when you walked in, whether or not you book a treatment.

You leave with a written plan: recommended treatments, timing, home care, and pricing. There is no pressure to start that day.
