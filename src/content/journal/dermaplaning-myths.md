Dermaplaning is one of our most requested treatments, and one of the most misunderstood.

## “My hair will grow back darker.”

Fine vellus hair grows back the same color and texture. Cutting hair does not change the follicle.

## “It’s just shaving.”

Dermaplaning also removes a layer of dead skin cells, using a sterile blade and technique designed for facial skin.

> Done well, it should leave skin smooth and calm, never irritated.
