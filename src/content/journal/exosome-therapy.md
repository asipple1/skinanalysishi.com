Think of your skin cells as tiny busy workers that are always talking to each other. They send signals, share instructions, and coordinate repairs — constantly. Exosomes are the messaging capsules that make that communication possible.

## What are exosomes?

Exosomes are microscopic structures released by cells. They carry proteins, lipids, and genetic material from one cell to another, essentially delivering instructions that tell neighboring cells how to behave. When we apply exosomes to the skin during a treatment, we are flooding the area with regenerative signals that tell your skin to produce collagen, repair damage, and restore balance.

## Why exosomes over other regenerative treatments?

Treatments derived from living cells — like PRP or certain stem cell therapies — carry a small but real risk of reaction because they contain complete biological material. Exosomes are cell-free. They deliver the growth factors and regenerative instructions without the cellular component, which significantly reduces the risk of adverse response.

The result is a treatment that can stimulate remarkable repair with very little inflammation or downtime.

## What exosome therapy addresses

- Fine lines and early wrinkles
- Post-procedure recovery (we frequently pair it with microneedling)
- Uneven skin tone and texture
- Dryness and compromised skin barrier
- Scarring, including acne scarring

## What to expect

Exosome therapy is often performed immediately after a microneedling session. The tiny channels created by microneedling allow the exosomes to penetrate deeply, amplifying the results of both treatments. On its own, exosome therapy is non-invasive — applied topically and absorbed into the skin.

Expect some initial redness that resolves within 24 hours. Most clients notice improved skin quality over the following weeks as collagen production ramps up.
