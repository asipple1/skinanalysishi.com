Dysport is one of our most requested treatments — and for good reason. It is FDA-approved, fast-acting, and when done well, the results look completely natural. Here is what you should know before your first appointment.

## What is Dysport?

Dysport is an injectable containing botulinum toxin type A. It temporarily relaxes the facial muscles responsible for dynamic wrinkles — the lines that form from repeated expressions like squinting, frowning, and smiling. It does not freeze your face. It softens the movement just enough to let the skin smooth out.

## Five things Dysport does well

**1. It works quickly.**
Most clients see results within two to three days. Full effect is visible by one week.

**2. The results look natural.**
Dysport has a slightly wider diffusion pattern than some other neuromodulators, which gives it a softer, more blended result — particularly in the forehead and crow's feet areas.

**3. It lasts.**
Results typically last three to four months. Many clients find that with consistent treatment, their results extend over time.

**4. There is no real downtime.**
The appointment takes 10 to 20 minutes. You can return to your day immediately. We recommend avoiding intense exercise and heat for 24 hours after, but that is it.

**5. It treats more than wrinkles.**
Dysport is also effective for hyperhidrosis — excessive sweating — and can be used in multiple areas beyond the face.

## What the appointment looks like

We start with a brief consultation to discuss your concerns and goals. We apply a topical anesthetic if needed, then make precise, small injections into the target muscles. The whole process is fast and well-tolerated.

> Done correctly, Dysport should leave you looking like yourself — just more rested.
