    a: "Most people find Dysport very tolerable. The injections are made with a fine needle and the treatment moves quickly, usually done in under 30 minutes. Bruising is possible but not common. We’ll walk you through a short list of things to avoid in the first 24 hours, like strenuous exercise and lying flat, to help protect your results."

Dysport tends to take effect slightly faster for some people and can spread a bit more, which can be an advantage in certain areas. Ultimately, the most important factor is how treatment is tailored to your anatomy and goals, not the brand."
